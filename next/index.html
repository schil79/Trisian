What people askbefore they call.

What do you actually do?

We run your organization's whole digital environment: the desktops your people work on, the security around them, and the support when something breaks. One partner, one contract. We work with healthcare, financial services and legal organizations.

What exactly is Safe Workspace?

A managed cloud desktop on Azure Virtual Desktop. It bundles Microsoft 365, security, monitoring and support into one service that we run for you. Your team logs in, and we handle everything underneath.

When our desktops get rebuilt, what happens to our files?

They stay. Profiles and files live in separate, managed storage, so people sign in to a fresh desktop with their work where they left it. Only the desktop gets rebuilt.

Which frameworks do you support?

Our workspace helps support CIS Controls v8.1, NIST CSF 2.0 and HIPAA on Advanced, and adds SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001 and HITRUST on Enterprise. We provide the technical controls. You own your compliance program and your auditor. On the first call, we'll show you where each framework fits.

How does pricing work?

Three lines. A per-user monthly platform fee that covers everything automated, including Microsoft 365 licensing. Contracted support hours for the human work, every hour logged. And projects, scoped and quoted separately. Azure is billed at cost, with no markup.

Do you replace our IT team?

No. We work with your team, not around it. Inside what we manage, we own the outcome. Your team keeps the context, and we take on the load.

How do we know which plan fits?

We work it out together on the first call. We look at your size, your industry and your hours, and recommend a starting point.

What happens outside business hours?

Every plan has emergency escalation through your named contacts, billed at an emergency rate. Core support runs during business hours, and we'll walk you through the extended coverage on Advanced and Enterprise on the call.

How long does it take to go live?

It depends on your environment. We scope the timeline with you up front, so you get a realistic plan, not a generic promise.

Can you move us off Citrix or VMware?

Yes. We migrate you from Citrix and VMware Horizon to Azure Virtual Desktop, keeping your profiles and settings. Migrations are scoped and quoted as projects.

Do you support our applications?

We manage the infrastructure and operating system your applications run on. Support for the applications themselves stays with you or your software vendor.

What happens on the first call?

It's a conversation about your environment and your goals. If we look like a fit, the next step is a closer look at your setup, and we tell you where you stand and which plan fits.
