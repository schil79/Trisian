Three lines on the invoice.Nothing hiding.

You always know what you're paying for, what's included and what's billable before any work begins.

Platform fee

Per user, per month. Everything that runs on its own is in it: monitoring, patching, security updates, Microsoft 365 licensing.

Support hours

A contracted minimum for the work people do. Every hour logged, categorized and reported monthly.

Projects

Migrations, security builds, compliance programs. Scoped up front, quoted separately.

Questions.

How does pricing work?

Three lines. A per-user monthly platform fee that covers everything automated, including Microsoft 365 licensing. Contracted support hours for the human work, every hour logged. And projects, scoped and quoted separately. Azure is billed at cost, with no markup.

How do we know which level fits?

The assessment decides. We score your security, performance and compliance against your user count, your industry and your regulators, then recommend a starting point and show our reasoning.

Can we move up later?

Yes. The architecture is the same at every level, so moving from Core to Advanced or Enterprise doesn’t mean a redesign.

What happens outside business hours?

Advanced and Enterprise include 24/7/365 support at the same rate. Every level has emergency escalation through your named contacts.

Have the answerbefore the meeting.

We review your environment against the rules you answer to. Then we tell you where the day-after meeting would go badly today.
