Questions

Where do client files live when the desktop gets rebuilt?

Not on the desktop. Files and profiles are stored separately from the session, so the rebuild clears the workspace and keeps the work.

Do you work with our practice-management software?

We manage the infrastructure and operating system your applications run on. Support for the applications themselves stays with you or your software vendor.

What happens on the first call?

It's a conversation about your environment and your goals. If we look like a fit, the next step is a closer look at your setup, and we tell you where you stand and which plan fits.
