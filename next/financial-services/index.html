The questions come all at once.

After an incident, a financial firm has a lot of people to answer to at the same time: clients, examiners, carriers, partners. Each one wants the same things: what happened, who was affected and when you knew. It's a lot easier to answer when the logs and the access list are already in order.

We rebuild our desktops from a clean copy on a regular schedule, so nothing unwanted piles up. Client files and profiles are kept apart from the desktop and protected on their own.
