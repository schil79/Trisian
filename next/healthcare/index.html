After an incident, it comes down to the timeline.

The first thing everyone wants after an incident is a timeline: what happened, when, and who knew. Most practices spend that meeting piecing it together from logs they have to go find. We'd rather you spend it deciding what to say.

Three questions. Three answers.

We rebuild our desktops from a clean copy on a regular schedule, so nothing unwanted piles up. Patient files and profiles are kept apart from the desktop and protected on their own.

Not on the desktop. Files and profiles are stored separately from the session, so the rebuild clears the workspace and keeps the work.
